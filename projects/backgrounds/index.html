Growing backgrounds

✹ Oct 24✬ Rio de Janeiro, BR000 view(s)

This site has four backgrounds that grow on a page while you read it. Each one draws on a canvas behind the page, starts when the page is ready, and stops when the window is full. A key in the frontmatter of a page turns a background on, and all four can grow on the same page, as on this one. With reduced motion, each background shows its finished drawing at once.

Plum #

Thin branches start at random places on the edges of the window and fork as they grow. Plum comes from the site of Anthony Fu, which this site started from.

Orchid #

Vines with curls, buds and plum blossoms grow from the bottom and the sides. Their shapes come from the ornament on the cover of the Suzuno Financial brochure in the series Tokyo Vice, which the Suzuno differences recreate. Each tip grows at its own uneven pace, the stems wander, and far vines are lighter and smaller than near ones. No two blossoms overlap.

Misty rose #

Misty rose grows the rose vines of Hammerfight (Konstantin Koshutin, 2009), which grow on the black screens between the levels of its story. The growth repeats the game step by step with the sprites of the game, used with the permission of its author. mistyrosetitle: true and the MistyRoseTitle component also let vines grow from titles, as in the note on misty rose.

Honeydew #

Snakes of あべっ子ラムネ candy packets crawl in from the edges, one packet at a time. The packets are the recreations from the candy differences, drawn once as images. The snakes keep apart from each other, and far snakes are smaller and lighter.

Usage #

---
plum: true
orchid: true
mistyrose: true
mistyrosetitle: true
honeydew: true
---

A missing key turns its background off. The components are Plum, Orchid, MistyRose and Honeydew in src/components/, and they share one canvas setup in useBackgroundCanvas.
